Tualatin Development Commission

The City's urban-renewal agency. Governed by City Council acting as the TDC Board, but with a budget that is legally separate from the City's. Tax-increment revenue collected within designated urban-renewal districts is restricted to capital improvements and economic development within those districts.

Urban Renewal Agency 6 funds · $8.05M FY 26-27 ↗ TDC Summary (p. 314)
Urban renewal Oregon urban-renewal agencies (ORS Ch. 457) raise revenue from tax-increment financing — the property-tax growth above a frozen base inside each district. That revenue stays inside the district and pays for capital improvements, infrastructure, and public-private investment. The TDC reports its budget under the same Local Budget Law cycle as the City but is its own taxing district with its own funds, debt, and reserves. See how state budget law applies →

TDC funds

Two urban-renewal districts (Southwest URD and Core Opportunity & Reinvestment Area) each have a Bond fund for proceeds + debt service and a Project fund for capital spending. Plus a small Administration fund and the closed Leveton Projects fund.

TDC fund balances reflect the FY 2026–27 proposed budget's Changes in Fund Balance schedule (printed p. 58). Funds with dashes for begin/change/end either had no activity in the prior year or report only project-level balances; see the linked PDF section for full detail.
