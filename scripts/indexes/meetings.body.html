Budget Advisory Committee meetings

What came up for discussion at each meeting of Tualatin's citizen Budget Advisory Committee (BAC) — the body that reviews the City Manager's proposed budget before City Council adopts it. Each meeting links to the official agenda, packet, and minutes; meetings with a "Read full minutes" link have the complete minutes quoted on an internal page.

↗ BAC home ↗ All BAC meetings ↗ Notice of budget hearings
State law Under Oregon Local Budget Law (ORS Ch. 294), every local budget moves through a nine-step cycle. The Budget Committee meetings in this list cover step 4 (committee meets to receive the budget message and hear from the public) and step 5 (committee approves the budget); the City Council public hearing that follows is step 7, and adoption by resolution before June 30 is step 8. FY 2026–27 cycle

Four scheduled meetings. The committee elects a chair, hears staff overviews, takes department presentations, and holds a public hearing before recommending the budget to Council.

February 9, 2026 · 5:00 p.m. minutes
Parks Utility Fee restructuring took up most of the meeting. FCS Consultants presented three rate scenarios — $5.50, $6.00, and $6.50 per EDU monthly (the current $5 has been unchanged since 2021). Discussion: Pratt flagged the missing inflation adjustment in the original rate as an oversight; Hannam and Brune favored $6.00; Sanchez-Velazquez urged the lowest option citing resident financial strain; Bachhuber raised the cumulative tax-and-rate-increase concern and asked about the upcoming water rate hike (Hudson confirmed ~11% on July 1). Staff also presented FY 25-26 budget-to-date, a Gaps & Needs Project update by Merina+Co, and legislative tracking.

Brittany Valli elected Chair. FY 23-24 proposed budget of $152.8M presented (3.55% increase). Recap of the $25M Parks Bond passage and the first $15M bond sale at premium ($17.1M for projects). Assessed-value growth of 15.63% driven by bond levies. New Parks Bond Project Manager position discussed.

About this listing

Tualatin publishes Budget Advisory Committee agendas, packets, and minutes through the Municode Meetings system. Document URLs follow the pattern mccmeetings.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/tualtnor-pubu/MEET-{Agenda|Packet|Minutes}-{meeting-uuid}.pdf. Standalone minutes PDFs exist for the FY 22-23 cycle and earlier; from FY 23-24 onward the city stopped publishing standalone minutes and instead embeds them inside the next year's kickoff packet. Pre-2020 meetings predate the Municode system and aren't in this listing.

Recordings are produced by Tualatin Valley Community Television (tvctv.org/tualatin); video links are not published per meeting.
