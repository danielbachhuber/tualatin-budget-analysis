Budget Advisory Committee meetings

What came up at each meeting of Tualatin's citizen Budget Advisory Committee (BAC) — the body that reviews the City Manager's proposed budget before City Council adopts it. Cards show the role the meeting played in the cycle, a one-glance summary, and links to the agenda, packet, and minutes.

↗ BAC home ↗ All BAC meetings ↗ Notice of budget hearings
State law Under Oregon Local Budget Law (ORS Ch. 294), every local budget moves through a nine-step cycle. The Budget Committee meetings in this list cover step 4 (committee meets to receive the budget message and hear from the public) and step 5 (committee approves the budget); the City Council public hearing that follows is step 7, and adoption by resolution before June 30 is step 8. Tualatin publishes BAC agendas, packets, and minutes through the Municode Meetings system at mccmeetings.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/tualtnor-pubu/MEET-{Agenda|Packet|Minutes}-{uuid}.pdf. Standalone minutes PDFs exist for the FY 22–23 cycle and earlier; from FY 23–24 onward minutes are embedded in the next year's kickoff packet. Pre-2020 meetings predate Municode. Recordings are produced by tvctv.org/tualatin; video links are not published per meeting.
