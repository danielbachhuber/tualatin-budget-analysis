FY 25-26 highlights p. 186

  • Used Parks Bond funding for investigative work on the Riverfront Park and to start construction at Nyberg Creek Trail.
  • Completed Victoria Woods stairs, Little Woodrose Trail, and Community Park shelter repair using the parks utility fee.
  • ARPA funding supported Las Casitas Park renovation and design of the Basalt Creek linear park.
  • Juanita Pohl Center meal program shift drove >300% increase in meal program participation; overall JPC participation grew 27% to over 16,000 visits.
  • 570 youth in summer camp with 39 scholarships provided.
  • Three service-inspired sculptures installed at Veterans Plaza after two years of planning.

Objectives for FY 26-27 p. 186

  • Facilitate community engagement for the future Riverfront Park; begin first stage of design.
  • Engage department- and community-wide on park projects and recreational uses of new parks, trails, and facilities.
  • Incorporate environmentally friendly features that value and protect natural resources, wildlife, and habitats.
  • Continue good value for recreation programs while phasing in market-based fee adjustments for sustainability.
  • Use new branding to deliver frequent, high-value communication across print, social, and in-person.
  • Build on Juanita Pohl Center meal program with more drop-in programs and meal-program enhancement.
