Parks Utility Fee Fund

The Parks Utility Fee collects funds to be used for improvements and replacements of aging infrastructure throughout the parks system. Identified projects are analyzed by Parks and Recreation Staff and the Tualatin Parks and Recreation Advisory Committee on an annual basis.
