Core Opportunity & Reinvestment Area Bond

The bond fund receives proceeds from tax increment generated revenue and holds it until such time enough revenue is generated to spend on projects and programs. In 2022, the City established an opportunity and reinvestment area in the northern section of the community called the Core Opportunity and Reinvestment Area (CORA).
