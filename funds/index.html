Overview · Funds

Funds

Every fund in the FY 2026–27 proposed budget, grouped by purpose. Beginning balance plus net change equals ending balance; a negative change means the city is intentionally spending down accumulated reserves on planned capital work.

19 city funds + TDC funds ↗ Changes in Fund Balance (p. 58)

General & supporting funds

The General Fund is the only unrestricted fund. Supporting funds handle revenue streams with restrictions on use.

Transportation

Operating, maintenance, and developer-impact funds for streets and the right-of-way.

Parks

Operating, system-development, and bond-financed funds supporting the parks system.

Utilities (Water · Sewer · Stormwater)

Enterprise-fund pairs — each utility runs an Operating fund (rates & ops) and a Development fund (SDCs for growth-related capital).

Special-purpose & one-time

Bond proceeds, federal grants, internal-service funds, and small dedicated funds.

Tualatin Development Commission

The city's urban-renewal agency, governed by City Council but with a legally separate budget. TDC Summary p. 314

All fund balances reflect the FY 2026–27 proposed budget's Changes in Fund Balance schedule (page 58). The Parks Utility Fee Fund has a full deep-dive page; the others link directly to their section in the source PDF.
