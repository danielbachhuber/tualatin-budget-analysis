Tualatin Scholarship Fund

The Tualatin Science & Technology Scholarship recognizes high-achieving Tualatin students who plan to pursue a degree in science, technology, engineering, math, or a related field at an Oregon college or university. Funded by a trust, the scholarship's goal is to support the next generation of innovation in STEM by providing educational opportunities to Tualatin students.
