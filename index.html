Proposed Budget · Fiscal Year July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027

A $176.9 million budget — what's inside, where the money comes from, and how it compares to the past seven years.

The City Manager's proposed FY 2026-27 budget is 6.8% larger than FY 2025-26 adopted, with most of the increase concentrated in capital projects. This page summarizes the proposed budget, charts seven years of revenue and expenditure trends, and links directly to every source document.

What's notable in the proposed budget

The City Manager's budget message and the "Summary of All Funds" highlight a handful of shifts that explain most of the year-over-year change.

Where the money comes from

Seven years of revenue by source. Beginning fund balance is shown separately because it represents money carried forward from prior years rather than new revenue.

Revenue by source

Stacked bars exclude beginning fund balance. FY26-27 values are proposed; all prior years are adopted budgets.

Total resources (incl. beginning fund balance)

Total resources equal total requirements by design — Oregon municipal budget law requires a balanced budget.

Where the money goes

Spending by object category. Contingencies & reserves include money the City sets aside but does not plan to spend in the fiscal year.

Spending by object

Categories follow the Budget in Brief schema; the FY26-27 proposed budget's finer-grained categories have been mapped to match (Franchise Fees folded into Licenses, Permits & Fees; Contingency + Reserves & Unappropriated combined).

Operating vs. capital

Operating = Personal Services + Materials & Services. Capital = Capital Outlay. Other categories (transfers, debt service, reserves) are excluded.

Methodology

PDFs were downloaded locally; text extracted with pypdf. Revenue and expenditure totals were transcribed from each year's Budget in Brief table (FY 20-21 through FY 25-26) and from the FY 26-27 proposed budget's Summary of All Funds. Where the proposed budget uses finer-grained revenue categories, this site normalizes them to the Budget in Brief schema for consistent trend lines.
