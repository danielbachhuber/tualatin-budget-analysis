What's driving this year's budget

Budget Message

Tualatin remains fiscally stable, but the budget is tightening — expenditure growth is outpacing revenue growth. Executive Team conversations resulted in not funding every departmental need, but maintained organizational stability. Capital Outlay is the largest expenditure line this year (34.4% of the budget), driven by water and sewer infrastructure.
