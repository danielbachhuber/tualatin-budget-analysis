What's driving this year's budget

Tualatin remains fiscally stable, but the budget is tightening — expenditure growth is outpacing revenue growth. Executive Team conversations resulted in not funding every departmental need, but maintained organizational stability. Capital Outlay is the largest expenditure line this year (34.4% of the budget), driven by water and sewer infrastructure.

Separate budget

Tualatin Development Commission

$8.05M FY 26-27 · 6 funds

The Tualatin Development Commission is the City's urban-renewal agency. The City Council acts as the TDC Board, but TDC's budget is legally separate from the City's — it has its own revenues (tax-increment financing), its own funds, and its own bond debt. It pays for capital improvements inside two designated urban-renewal districts: Southwest URD and the Core Opportunity & Reinvestment Area (CORA).
