FY 2026–27 cycle

Four scheduled meetings. The committee elects a chair, hears staff overviews, takes department presentations, and holds a public hearing before recommending the budget to Council.

February 9, 2026 · 5:00 p.m. ↗ minutes
Parks Utility Fee restructuring took up most of the meeting. FCS Consultants presented three rate scenarios — $5.50, $6.00, and $6.50 per EDU monthly (the current $5 has been unchanged since 2021). Committee discussion: Pratt flagged the missing inflation adjustment in the original rate as an oversight; Hannam and Brune favored $6.00; Sanchez-Velazquez urged the lowest option citing resident financial strain; Bachhuber raised the cumulative tax-and-rate-increase concern and asked about the upcoming water rate hike (Hudson confirmed ~11% on July 1). Staff also presented the FY 25-26 budget-to-date (trending as expected), a Gaps & Needs Project update by Merina+Co, and legislative tracking (SB 1586 JOBS Act, HB 4084, TLT bills).

FY 2025–26 cycle for comparison

Last year's meetings — useful for tracking what topics recurred and what the committee asked staff to follow up on.

May 12, 2025 · kickoff ↗ minutes
Elizabeth Michels elected chair. Hudson presented the $165.1M proposed budget overview (10.53% revenue growth on a 3% AV growth assumption). Tanous asked about federal EV charging grant status, Climate Action Plan funding (confirmed included after the prior year's $100K motion failed), and bicycle access (staff to follow up).

May 28, 2025 · public hearing & adoption ↗ minutes
Public hearing on state liquor tax ($479,130). Internal Services department presentations. Public comment from Friends of Tualatin Library president Laura Baker on the WCCLS levy reduction — the next levy funds only 45 hrs/wk vs. current 65; Pratt asked if contingency could backfill (Lombos: current year intact, future years TBD). Hannam asked about the Water Fund project funding ($9M via rates). Dirksen raised utility rate increases. Committee approved the FY 25-26 budget at $2.2665/$1,000 operating rate plus $5,263,160 GO bond debt service. Carryovers included replacement of the stolen "Girl with the Ice Cream Cone" statue.

About this listing

Tualatin publishes Budget Advisory Committee meeting agendas, packets, and minutes through the Municode Meetings system. Each document URL follows the pattern mccmeetings.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/tualtnor-pubu/MEET-{Agenda|Packet|Minutes}-{meeting-uuid}.pdf. The UUID is shared across all three document types for the same meeting, so once a meeting has been agendized you can predict the minutes URL once they post.

Recordings are produced by Tualatin Valley Community Television (tvctv.org/tualatin); video links are not published per meeting on the City site.
