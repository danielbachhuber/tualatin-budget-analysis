## Notes

- Adopted budgets are board-approved final budgets; proposed budgets are pre-adoption drafts presented to the Budget Committee.
- Tualatin's fiscal year runs July 1 – June 30. "FY2026-27" means July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027.
- PDFs are downloaded into `pdfs/` (gitignored) for local text extraction. URLs above are the canonical deep links for the microsite.
