TThe city manager has set before the budget committee a proposed budget of one hundred and seventy-six million, nine hundred and fifty-one thousand, three hundred and sixty dollars for the fiscal year commencing the first of July. It is larger than the budget adopted twelve months earlier by some six and eight tenths percent; the surplus is, in fact, concentrated chiefly in capital outlay—a sewer trunk-line expansion along Martinazzi Avenue and SW 108th, continued construction at the B-Level Reservoir, the new Aquifer Storage and Recovery well, and improvements to several neighborhood parks.

What follows is an attempt to present these figures with the deference such matters deserve—and the curiosity that public money invites.

On Method

Total resources and requirements figures come directly from each year's Budget in Brief table (FY 2020-21 through FY 2025-26) and from the FY 2026-27 proposed budget's Summary of All Funds.

PDFs were downloaded locally and text was extracted with pypdf; the figures were then transcribed and reconciled. All seven years balance to the dollar against published totals.

The FY 2026-27 proposed budget uses a slightly finer-grained revenue schema; figures shown here have been folded into the Budget in Brief categories so the trend lines are comparable.
